Carpools and everyday help with the families you know

Favorly is a private app for families, friends and neighbors you already know. Share rides to kids' activities and events, ask for an airport pickup, find someone to look after your pet, or borrow a tool. Favors keep the give and take fair.

Rides to activities and events

Offer seats for soccer practice, a school concert or a game. Parents choose a driver and a pickup option (at the driver's home, a meeting point, or door to door) and see their own pickup time. Follow the ride live, only while it's happening.

Private by invitation

There's no public directory. People join with their phone number when someone they know invites them.

Pet care and tool lending

Ask trusted friends to board your pet or drop in, with care updates. Lend and borrow tools with simple pickup and return.

Favorly is in a private pilot. If a family you know invited you, you'll get a test invite for iPhone (TestFlight) or Android (Google Play).

Support

Questions, problems, or feedback about the app? Email us at support.favorly@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I join? Someone you know adds your phone number to an activity or event. Sign up with that number and it will be waiting for you.

Who can see my location? Only the families on a ride you're driving, and only from when you tap Start trip until you end the trip.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Me → Settings & privacy → Delete account, or see Delete your account.
