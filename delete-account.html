Delete your Favorly account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Favorly and go to Me.
  2. Tap Settings & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email support.favorly@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my Favorly account" and the phone number you signed up with. We'll send a code to that phone number to confirm it's you, then delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, photo, phone number and home address.
  • Children's profiles, pets, vehicle and tools you added, if no one else in your household still uses the app.
  • Trip location history.
  • Your chat messages are removed or shown as "Deleted member".

What we keep, and for how long

  • Favors history entries that involve other households are kept without your name so their balances stay correct.
  • Records we must keep by law, for up to the period the law requires.

Before you delete

If your household has Favors left, send them to someone first, or they will be lost. If your household owes Favors, please settle them before deleting.
