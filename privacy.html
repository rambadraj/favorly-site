Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Applies to the Favorly app during its pilot in the United States.

Favorly ("we") helps families, friends and neighbors who know each other share rides, airport pickups, pet care and tools. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Who uses Favorly

Accounts are for adults 18 and older. Children do not have accounts and do not use the app. Parents may add limited information about their children so other parents can drive them safely (see below).

Information we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Phone numberTo create your account and sign you in with a one-time code, and so people you know can invite you.
Name and optional photoSo other members of your activities know who you are.
Home addressTo plan routes, work out distances, pickup times and Favors. It is required to finish signing up.
Children's first names, grade, and optional notes (for example allergies, booster seat, emergency contact)Entered by a parent so drivers can keep children safe.
Vehicle details (make, model, color, plate, seats)So families know which car to look for.
Location during tripsWhen you drive and tap Start trip, your phone's location is shared with the families on that ride so they can follow it. Sharing stops when you end the trip, or automatically an hour after the scheduled end.
Activities, rides, requests, chat messages, pet and tool detailsTo run the features you use.
Favors historyTo keep a record of help given and received.
Contacts (only if you choose to invite from contacts)To let you pick people to invite. Contacts are matched on your phone and are not uploaded to our servers.
Device and diagnostic data (app version, crash reports)To fix problems and keep the app reliable.

We do not collect your location when you aren't driving an active trip, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your information

  • Members of your activities and events see your name, photo, the children you add to that activity, and the rides you offer or book.
  • Your home address is shared only with the driver of a ride where your child is picked up at home. A driver's address is shared only with families who choose to drop off at the driver's home.
  • Children's safety notes are shown only to the driver of a trip that child is on.
  • Live location is visible only to the families on that ride, only during the trip.
  • People asking to join an activity see only its name, time, number of families and organizer's first name, never members, children or addresses.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the app, under contracts that limit their use of your data to providing their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, data storage, notifications) and Google Cloud (database).
  • Google Maps Platform (address lookup and distances).
  • An SMS provider (invite texts).
  • Apple and Google (app distribution and push notifications).

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Detailed trip location points are deleted after 30 days. We keep only the trip summary (distance and times).
  • Account information is kept while your account is open and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account, except records we must keep by law.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile, address, children and vehicle at any time in the app.
  • You can turn off notifications and remove contacts access in your phone's settings.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Me → Settings & privacy → Delete account) or as described on Delete your account.
  • California and other US state residents may ask to access, correct or delete their information. Email us and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

Children's privacy

Favorly is not directed to children and children cannot create accounts. Information about children is provided by their parent or guardian, used only to coordinate their rides and care, and shown only as described above. A parent can edit or remove their child's information at any time. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by membership rules so people see only what their activities need.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app if the change is significant.

Contact

Email support.favorly@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

This policy covers the Favorly pilot. It will be reviewed before the app is released publicly.
